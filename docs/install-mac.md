# Install the Captions app on a Mac

The Captions app puts live captions on top of everything on the projector screen. It's free
and open source. It isn't registered with Apple (that costs $99 a year), so macOS asks you to
confirm it once.

**Needs:** macOS 12 or later. About 5 MB.

## 1. Download

Download **Captions.app.zip** from the latest release on the project's GitHub page
(*Releases*, on the right), then double-click it to unzip.
Drag **Captions** into your **Applications** folder.

## 2. Open it the first time

1. Double-click **Captions**. macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer".
   Click **Done** (not "Move to Bin").
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and scroll down to **Security**.
   You'll see *"Captions" was blocked…*. Click **Open Anyway**, then enter your password.
3. Double-click **Captions** again and click **Open**.

You only do this once per Mac.

## 3. Use it

- A **CC** icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. There's no window and no Dock icon.
- The room code and QR code appear on the projector screen until your phone connects.
  Scan the QR code with your phone, or open the web address on your phone and type the code.
- Tap **Start captions** on your phone. Captions appear at the bottom of the projector.

**The CC menu:**

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Show room code on screen | Shows the code card again, for a new phone |
| Captions position: Top / Bottom | Moves the captions. Shortcut: **Ctrl+Option+Cmd+↑ / ↓** |
| Show on | Which screen. "Projector (automatic)" picks the screen that isn't the Mac's own |
| Move out of the pointer's way | If your pointer rests over the captions for a second, they jump to the other edge |
| Hide captions | Shortcut: **Ctrl+Option+Cmd+C** |
| Open at login | Start Captions automatically when you log in |

## If something isn't right

- **Captions on the wrong screen:** CC menu → Show on → pick the projector.
- **Edges cut off on the projector:** set the Mac's display resolution for the projector to a 16:10 mode
  (System Settings → Displays → the projector → Show all resolutions → 1280 × 800).
- **Nothing appears:** check that your phone says *Screen: connected* and that you tapped **Start captions**.
  The captions only show while a lesson is live.
